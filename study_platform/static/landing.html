Image Quality Study

Duration ≈ 25–30 minutes · Compensation per your recruitment channel · Anonymous

Purpose

You will view photographs on this website and rate how bothersome the lost highlight/shadow detail (blown-out or crushed areas) is in each photograph, on a scale from 0 (not at all) to 100 (extremely bothersome).

What you must do

  • View each image for at least ~2 seconds before answering.
  • Rate your immediate impression; there is no right answer.
  • Complete the session in one sitting, in a quiet place, without zooming.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time; your data will then be deleted. No personally identifying information is collected. Data are stored anonymously and used only for scientific analysis of image-quality assessment.

Requirements

  • Normal or corrected-to-normal vision (glasses/contacts are fine).
  • A display used indoors; avoid direct sunlight on the screen.
  • Viewing distance of roughly 50–70 cm (arm's length).
